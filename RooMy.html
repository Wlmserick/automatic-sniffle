RooMy

A safer, smarter way to find the ideal roommate and maintain harmony at home

Introduction

RooMy is a mobile platform that simplifies the process of finding compatible roommates and fosters a positive living environment once you move in together. With increasing urban density and changing life milestones, shared housing has become a permanent solution rather than a temporary one; it is now the new normal.

Solution

Create a safe, trustworthy roommate-matching platform

Focus on behavioral and lifestyle compatibility

Support long-term roommate harmony

Design for both roommate-seekers and apartment-seekers

Problem Discovery

As more people live with roommates for longer periods due to rising urban density and changing life milestones, the process of finding a compatible roommate remains unsafe, time-consuming, and stressful.


Shared housing is increasingly becoming a long-term arrangement rather than temporary, yet the existing roommate-finding methods do not adequately address safety, transparency, or ease throughout the entire roommate lifecycle. Can this be solved?

My Role

I managed the overall design process, focusing on product scoping, user flows, wireframes, rapid prototyping, and usability testing.


Additionally, I conducted user research and analyzed the findings to generate viable ideas.

Research Process

Feature Highlights

Smart Compatibility Matching

Lifestyle routines, cleanliness expectations, noise habits, guest policies, personality insights, and values are combined into a compatibility score.

Apartment & Roommate Matching

Search room listings or people looking for roommates using map-based discovery tools.

Verified Profiles

Government ID checks, phone verification, and facial match technology ensure trust and safety.

Roommate Harmony Tools

Shared bills, chores, house guidelines, and anonymous feedback check-ins help keep the home running smoothly.

AMA 26, Room Seeker

Needs: Verified profiles, transparent info, safe meetups, compatibility scores

TUNDE 30, Room Advertiser

Goals: Fill room quickly, avoid unreliable roommates

Pain Points: Ghosting, no-shows, lifestyle mismatches

Needs: Pre-qualified matches, scheduling automation, safe viewing process
